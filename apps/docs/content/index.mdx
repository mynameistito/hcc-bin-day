This guide explains how to use Hamilton Bin Day and what each collection week means. The schedule is looked up from the public Hamilton City Council Fight the Landfill service.

## Find your collection

1. Open the [address lookup](/) and enter your Hamilton street address.
2. Choose a suggested address if your search needs refining.
3. Check the next collection date, week colour, and bins to put out.

Collection dates can change around public holidays or due to council service updates. Check the council's [official waste and recycling information](https://www.hamilton.govt.nz/) if you need to confirm a change.

## Red and yellow weeks

| Week        | Containers                                             |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Red week    | Red rubbish bin and food scraps bin                    |
| Yellow week | Yellow recycling bin, glass crate, and food scraps bin |

Food scraps are collected every week. Set out the listed containers on your regular collection day, with lids closed and placed at the kerb according to council guidance.

## Data and privacy

Address searches are sent to Hamilton City Council's publicly available lookup API so the service can find a matching address and schedule. This site does not need an account. The project does not store the address search in its own database.

## About this project

Hamilton Bin Day is an unofficial community tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Hamilton City Council. Schedules are provided as a convenience; council information is authoritative. Browse the [project source on GitHub](https://github.com/mynameistito/hcc-bin-day).
